Package & Contract FAQs

Pertanyaan yang Sering Diajukan (FAQ)

Jawaban transparan untuk pertanyaan seputar proses pengerjaan, biaya, kepemilikan, dan pemeliharaan website.

How do revision rounds work during development?

Each package includes dedicated structured revision milestones. Starter includes 2 comprehensive revision rounds, Business includes 4 rounds, and Enterprise provides unlimited iterative refinement until the staging preview is officially approved.

Are domain names and cloud hosting included in the package?

You may connect your own existing domain and hosting at no extra charge, or request our integrated turnkey package (including .com/.id domain registration and guaranteed fast NVMe Cloud SSD hosting).

Are there any recurring monthly licensing fees?

Zero. We charge zero proprietary CMS software license fees or monthly code retainers. The website is your permanent digital asset. Future expenses are strictly standard annual registrar renewals (domain and hosting) directly payable to your registrar.

How do international payments and invoices work?

We generate official B2B corporate invoices and tax receipts. Payments can be settled via international SWIFT bank transfer, Wise Business, or corporate card in USD, SGD, or IDR.
